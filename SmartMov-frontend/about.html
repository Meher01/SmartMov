About SmartMov

SmartMov: Because Traditional To-Do Lists Are a Myth

Let's be honest: standard to-do lists are great for making us feel productive while we write them, and entirely useless when it comes to actually getting things done. We cram our days with unrealistic checkboxes, feel guilty when we miss half of them, and eventually abandon the list altogether.

I built SmartMov because I wanted something different. Instead of focusing on endless task completion, SmartMov focuses on pure, measurable learning time.


How SmartMov Solves the Problem

SmartMov shifts the paradigm from checking boxes to building actual study habits with a comprehensive suite of features:

  • Time Over Tasks: Instead of just checking off "Study Math," you set a daily minute goal. You don't fail a task; you just track your actual focused minutes.
  • Everything in One Place: No more jumping between browser tabs, PDFs, a note you would like to remember, and YouTube videos. You can attach all your learning resources directly to your targets and launch them instantly.
  • Built for Deep Work: It features a dedicated session window with paused-session recovery, so you can lock in without distractions.
  • Gamified Momentum: Building a habit is hard, so SmartMov rewards consistency. Creating targets keeps your daily streak alive, and every session earns you XP. Plus, your level automatically scales up as your streak grows (Level = [Streak / 3] + 1).

Under the Hood

While the concept is simple, the engineering is robust. SmartMov is powered by a Java 21 / Spring Boot backend with Spring Security (JWT) and MySQL for data persistence, paired with a clean, fast Vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript frontend that uses BroadcastChannel and browser popups for a seamless timer experience.


In short, SmartMov is designed to help you actually learn, not just feel like you're learning. It's about building habits, not checking boxes.
